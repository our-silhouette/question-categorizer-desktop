1. What is Question Categorizer?

Question Categorizer is a desktop application powered by Google's Gemini AI. It reads your question paper (PDF or Word file), matches each question to your curriculum's Knowledge Components (KCs), and produces a detailed Excel report — automatically.

5. Using the App — Step by Step

1

Enter Your API Key

In the left sidebar, under ⚙️ Configuration, paste your Gemini API key.

2

Upload Your Question Paper

Under 📄 Question File, click "Browse files" and select your question paper.

  • PDF — standard question paper PDFs
  • Word (.docx) — Microsoft Word documents
3

Upload Your KC / Syllabus File

Under 📚 Master Knowledge Components, upload your curriculum hierarchy file.

  • Excel (.xlsx) — KC hierarchy spreadsheet
  • Word (.docx) — structured KC document
4

Start Categorization

Click the blue "🚀 Start Categorization Pipeline" button. The AI will process your files — typically 1–3 minutes depending on question count and internet speed.

💡 What's happening

Each question is sent to Google's Gemini AI along with your KC structure. Gemini matches every question to the best-fitting KC and sub-KC, then returns the categorized result.

5

Download Your Results

When done, click "📥 Download Categorization Report (.xlsx)" to save your results as an Excel file.

6. Understanding Your Results

The downloaded Excel report contains one row per question:


Column Description
Question Number As it appears in the original paper
Question Text Full question text extracted by the AI
Topic / KC Top-level Knowledge Component matched to this question
Sub-KC Specific sub-topic within the KC
Confidence AI confidence level for the match
Marks Marks allocated (where available in the source document)

7. Closing the App

  1. Close the browser tab showing the Question Categorizer.
  2. Find the console window that opened at launch.
  3. Press Ctrl + C inside it, or simply close the window.
💡 Next time

Just double-click the app again — it starts a fresh session each time.

8. Troubleshooting & FAQ

❓ The browser didn't open automatically.
Look at the console window — it shows a URL like http://localhost:8501. Open your browser and type that address manually.
❓ I see an "Invalid API Key" error.
Re-copy the full key from aistudio.google.com — make sure there are no extra spaces before or after it.
❓ macOS says "cannot verify the developer" and won't open the app.
Right-click (or Control-click) the .app file → choose "Open" → click "Open" in the dialog. Only needed once.
❓ Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen).
Click "More info" → then "Run anyway". Standard warning for apps outside the Microsoft Store — safe to proceed.
❓ The app is slow / taking a long time.
Processing speed depends on your internet connection and question count. Large papers with 50+ questions may take 3–5 minutes. This is normal — Gemini AI is carefully reading and matching each question.
❓ I see a "quota exceeded" error.
The free tier has a daily request limit. Wait a few hours and try again, or check your quota at aistudio.google.com.
❓ My file format is not supported.
Question paper: PDF or Word (.docx). KC file: Excel (.xlsx) or Word (.docx). Convert your file to one of these before uploading.
❓ The app opened but the browser shows a blank or error page.
Wait 5–10 seconds and refresh the browser tab. The local server sometimes takes a moment on the very first launch.
